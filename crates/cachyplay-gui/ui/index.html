Settings

General

The tray icon lights up while a game runs, gets an amber dot while a fix is being tested or an update is ready, and a red dot after a crash. When it's off, CachyPlay tells you these with a desktop notification instead.

Privacy

CachyPlay works offline. The only things it ever sends: checking GitHub for updates (no personal details), and, if you turn it on below, asking ProtonDB how well a game runs for other players (only the game's number is sent). Nothing else leaves your PC, and bug reports stay on it until you share them.

Bug reports you saved

    PC check

    The things that most often stop games on Linux. Nothing is changed; where something needs fixing, there's a command to copy.

      OBS game capture

      Starts games through obs-gamecapture, so OBS's Game Capture source records just the game instead of your whole screen. You can also turn it on for one game in that game's Settings tab.

      Your game settings

      What CachyPlay applies to each game, written as Steam launch options. Paste a line into the game's Properties → Launch options in Steam to keep it without CachyPlay.

        Advanced

        Self-test

        Checks CachyPlay's own setup: where it's installed, and that Steam can find it.

          Restore all games

          Turns CachyPlay off for every game, on every Steam account, and puts their launch options back. Your own options stay.
